| البنك المركزي النيجر | النيجر (Niger) | https://www.bceao.int |

**Classification:** BCEAO is a **regional central bank** serving 8 West African countries (UMOA/WAEMU zone). These are NOT duplicate institutions — they are member states sharing a single central bank. The same institution (BCEAO) serves multiple jurisdictions.

**Treatment:** These should resolve to ONE institution (BCEAO) with MULTIPLE jurisdiction entries, NOT be deduplicated as duplicates. Entity resolution must recognize regional central bank coverage.
